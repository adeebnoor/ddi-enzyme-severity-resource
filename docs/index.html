Enzyme-Resolved Drug–Drug Interaction Mechanism–Severity Resource

Cytochrome-P450 and transporter mechanisms bridged to DDInter 2.0 clinical severity grades · 1,900 interaction records

What this is. Each row is a drug pair whose interaction mechanism is attributed to a specific enzyme or transporter (not a broad category), joined to its DDInter clinical-severity grade and flagged where the pair is independently observed in clinical-trial adverse-event reports. Methods & provenance. Mechanisms from D3/TWOSIDES enzyme-detail attribution (UniProt-identified CYP/transporter); severity from DDInter 2.0; compound identifiers bridged via PubChem (313/448 CIDs joined, 70%); trial corroboration from NCT-linked, UMLS-coded adverse-event reports. Per-enzyme odds ratios are within-overlap relative comparisons (this enzyme vs other enzymes), Benjamini–Hochberg-corrected. Released CC-BY 4.0. See ENZYME_RESOURCE_README.md for full detail and the counting convention (per-enzyme membership counts sum above the 562 unique non-Unknown pairs because a pair inhibiting several enzymes is counted once per enzyme).
