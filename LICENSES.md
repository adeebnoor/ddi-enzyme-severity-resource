# Scope of licences and unresolved source permissions

Author-written code is MIT. Original author-written documentation and figures are CC BY 4.0 within the rights owned by the author. These licences do not override third-party terms. No blanket CC BY permission is asserted for original source-derived annotations. Redistribution permission for the historical enzyme-attribution core has not been independently established; a component-scoped author licence does not supply that missing grant. `LICENSE` contains the licence texts and scope statement. A request for clarification of the applicable historical DrugBank terms and redistribution permission has been sent to DrugBank. The response is pending; no new permission is asserted.

DDInter clinical grades, raw trueDDI and DrugBank reference files, reference-membership pair flags, and raw KEGG and MIMIC validation exports are excluded. These exclusions do not certify permission for the source-derived core annotations. Authorised users obtain their own source files under the applicable terms. Reference reconstruction writes aggregate counts and an audit summary only. Clinical-trial flags and the incomplete HPO layer are withdrawn.

Exact author-held DrugBank and trueDDI snapshots were recovered and are identified by filename, SHA-256 and dated archival correspondence in `sources/recovered_reference_sources.csv`. Those dates do not establish export creation dates, provider releases, clinical independence or redistribution permission. Original D3 rule logic was also recovered; two historical graph candidates provide partial support while exact current-record derivation and source-specific permissions remain unestablished. The author-held GoldD3R export is an identifiable contextual export of D3 (article DOI 10.1093/jamia/ocw128); its deposited hash does not confer additional source rights. Public structural checks do not certify upstream licences.

This is dataset snapshot 2.3.0, published on Zenodo as DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23169653. The source manifest records known acquisition metadata and unresolved fields explicitly. The exact current UniProt accession snapshot is attributed to the UniProt Consortium, release 2026_03, retrieved 5 October 2026, under its CC BY 4.0 source terms (https://www.uniprot.org/help/license).

Archival publication records the snapshot but does not establish missing source permissions. Current DrugBank academic terms are not used as a retrospective licence grant for unidentified historical inputs. Post-publication citation and permission-status documentation updates leave the scientific files unchanged.
